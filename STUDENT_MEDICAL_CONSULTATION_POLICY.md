# BRS Student OPD Medical Consultation Policy

## Purpose
BRS Digital Library may offer an optional, discounted OPD medical consultation service exclusively to currently enrolled BRS students, intended to support their health and continuity of study and career growth.

**Student benefit:** ₹100 per eligible OPD consultation.

## Clinicians
- Dr Abhishek Bhatt — MBBS, DNB
- Dr Ishita Pandey — MBBS, DNB

## Eligibility
The ₹100 fee is a student benefit available only to an active/enrolled BRS student whose membership can be verified by the BRS administration.

It is not a public consultation offer and is not transferable to non-enrolled persons.

## Scope
The service is for selected non-emergency outpatient concerns, subject to clinician availability and clinical appropriateness. The clinician may advise in-person evaluation, investigations, referral, or emergency care where appropriate.

## Emergency disclaimer
This is NOT an emergency service. Severe or rapidly worsening symptoms require immediate assessment at the nearest appropriate emergency medical facility or local emergency service.

## Privacy
Medical information should be collected and handled separately from ordinary study/activity data and only to the extent necessary for the consultation, subject to applicable law and professional obligations.

## Fees
₹100 is the discounted consultation fee for enrolled BRS students. Investigations, medicines, procedures, hospital/clinic charges, specialist referrals, or other third-party costs are not included unless expressly stated.

## No guarantee
The consultation is not a guarantee of diagnosis, prescription, treatment outcome, or availability. Clinical decisions remain with the treating clinician based on the information available.

## Professional compliance
The service should operate in accordance with applicable Indian medical, telemedicine, privacy, record-keeping, and professional requirements. Final public launch wording should be reviewed against the current applicable rules and the clinicians' registration/credential requirements.

## Positioning
**For unhindered study days and career growth.**
