# BRS Legal & Safety Launch Checklist

Before public launch, fill in:
- [ ] Legal entity/operator name
- [ ] Registered/business address
- [ ] Support email and phone
- [ ] Grievance contact/officer details if applicable
- [ ] Privacy/data-protection contact if applicable
- [ ] Final governing law/jurisdiction clause
- [ ] Final refund/cancellation terms
- [ ] Final vendor/data-processor list
- [ ] Data retention schedule
- [ ] Security incident process
- [ ] Child/minor policy and verifiable guardian-consent mechanism
- [ ] Age-assurance method if minors are ever permitted
- [ ] Lawful process for emergency/law-enforcement requests
- [ ] Accountant/receipt process for cash memberships
- [ ] Tax/GST position reviewed by accountant
- [ ] Lawyer review of Terms, Privacy, Safety, Recording and Refund documents

## Recommended pilot position
Until these items are reviewed, operate the public pilot as adults-only (18+), with no recording by BRS and strict user recording/capture prohibition.
