1. Information collected

Depending on use, BRS may collect name, email, phone number, account credentials in hashed form, membership/expiry information, study-session timestamps and duration, room participation information, reports/complaints and technical/security logs.

2. Camera and microphone

Camera and microphone access is controlled by your browser and is optional in camera-optional rooms. BRS does not record live study video/audio by default. Users are prohibited from recording or redistributing other participants' feeds.

3. Purposes

Data may be used to provide accounts and membership, enable study rooms, maintain security, administer support, investigate safety reports, prevent abuse, comply with law and maintain operational records.

4. Data minimisation

Do not post sensitive personal information in rooms. BRS should collect only information reasonably necessary for the stated purposes and should establish retention periods before production launch.
