Community Safety Rules

Zero-tolerance areas: sexual content involving minors, grooming, harassment, threats, doxxing, blackmail, non-consensual recording, intimate-image abuse, impersonation, deepfake misuse and distribution of participant footage.

Users must

  • Use their own account and truthful identity information.
  • Keep camera/microphone use respectful and appropriate.
  • Respect other participants' privacy.
  • Report suspected abuse promptly through the BRS report mechanism.
  • Follow applicable law and BRS instructions from moderators/admins.

Users must not

  • Record, screenshot or redistribute another participant's feed.
  • Sexually harass, groom, threaten, bully or stalk anyone.
  • Share another person's phone number, address, private images or other personal information without lawful authority/consent.
  • Use another person's face/voice for impersonation, deepfakes or deceptive content.
  • Attempt to circumvent bans or create replacement accounts after enforcement.
  • Use the service for unlawful, fraudulent or harmful activity.

Enforcement

BRS may warn, mute, remove, suspend or permanently terminate accounts; restrict room access; preserve relevant information where lawful; and cooperate with lawful investigations. Serious safety issues may be escalated to parents/guardians where appropriate, emergency services or law-enforcement authorities.
