# BRS Safety Incident Response SOP

## Severity 1 — Immediate safety risk
Examples: child sexual exploitation/grooming, credible threat of violence, blackmail, intimate-image abuse, serious stalking.

1. Remove/suspend the account and room access where appropriate.
2. Preserve relevant technical/report information lawfully; do not alter evidence unnecessarily.
3. Escalate to the designated safety lead immediately.
4. Contact parent/guardian only where appropriate and lawful for a minor.
5. Contact law enforcement/emergency services when required or appropriate under law.
6. Record the incident, decision, time and person responsible.

## Severity 2 — Serious misconduct
Harassment, non-consensual recording, doxxing, impersonation, repeated bullying.

1. Restrict access while reviewing.
2. Review report and available logs.
3. Decide warning, temporary suspension or termination.
4. Document the decision.

## Severity 3 — Routine abuse
Spam, minor rule violations, inappropriate behaviour.

1. Warn or moderate.
2. Repeat violations may lead to suspension.

## Evidence and privacy
Collect only what is reasonably necessary. Do not publicly expose reporter identity. Do not promise absolute confidentiality where disclosure may be legally required.

## Contact
Insert the final safety/grievance email, responsible officer and escalation contacts before launch.
