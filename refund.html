Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026.

Pilot plans

BRS may offer ₹250/month and ₹650/3 months. During the pilot, offline/cash memberships are activated manually.

Cancellation

Users may request cancellation through the published support channel. Cancellation stops future renewal/activation; it does not automatically create a refund for a period already used.

Refunds

For unused periods, duplicate payments, technical failure attributable to BRS or other situations where a refund is required by applicable law, BRS will assess the request fairly and issue an appropriate refund where applicable. Any consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded remain unaffected.

Misconduct

Accounts may be suspended/terminated for serious violations. Any treatment of unused membership balances will be handled consistently with applicable law and the final terms.

Offline payments

For cash payments, BRS should issue/maintain a receipt or transaction record showing amount, plan, date and membership expiry. Do not collect cash without a record.
