Recording, Screenshots & Video Misuse Notice

Strict rule: BRS does not authorize users to record, screenshot, screen-capture, photograph, stream, rebroadcast, download, archive, edit, remix, publish or redistribute another participant's camera feed, voice, image, name, profile or room activity.

Prohibited conduct

  • Recording or capturing a participant without their informed permission and a lawful basis.
  • Uploading or sharing BRS room footage on social media, messaging groups, websites or any public/private channel.
  • Using a participant's face, voice or likeness for impersonation, harassment, deepfakes, sexualized content, advertising or commercial exploitation.
  • Doxxing, threats, blackmail, stalking or attempts to identify a participant from room activity.
  • Bypassing technical controls or using third-party tools to capture protected content.

Enforcement

BRS may immediately suspend or terminate access, preserve relevant logs where lawful, investigate reports, cooperate with lawful requests from authorities and seek other remedies available under applicable law. Nothing in this notice prevents a user from complying with a lawful order or exercising rights available under law.

Important limitation

Browser technology cannot guarantee that a user cannot make an external recording (for example, with another phone). Users must therefore rely on the contractual rules, reporting system and applicable law. BRS does not promise that misuse is technically impossible.
